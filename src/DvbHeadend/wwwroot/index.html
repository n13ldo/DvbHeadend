Channel scan

Tunes every mux below and rebuilds the channel list, using the broadcaster's channel numbers. Your renames, favourites, logos and hidden channels are kept.


      

      

        
Channel logos

        
Logos are yours to supply. Pick several image files at once: a file named after the channel number (1.png) or the channel name (BBC ONE Scot.png) is matched automatically. One logo at a time can also be set from a channel's Edit button. PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB each.

        
        

      

      

        
Home Assistant (MQTT)

        

        
Set the broker under Headend:Mqtt in /etc/dvbheadend/settings.json and restart the service. Tuners, viewing and per-mux signal then appear in Home Assistant as a DvbHeadend device.
